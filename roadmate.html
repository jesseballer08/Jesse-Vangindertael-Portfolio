RoadMate

UX / Product design · Figma · Team project

RoadMate is an MVP I designed together with Senne Robbrecht and Soulaimane Achkif for the Digital Experience Design course, in collaboration with VIAS Institute. Many drivers forget or ignore parts of the highway code after passing their exam, so RoadMate refreshes that knowledge during the ride itself — an AI voice reads short traffic-code trivia at set intervals, with a quick quiz afterwards to check what stuck.

Process

01

Drivers rarely revisit the highway code after their exam, leading to forgotten rules and avoidable mistakes. RoadMate turns the drive itself into the refresher, with no separate study time needed.

Concept

02

We built personas for three driver types and ran a survey among 63 drivers: most preferred short, 5-to-10-second audio updates and felt traffic rules don't get enough attention after their exam.

Content

03

A crossroads mark in charcoal blue and a sunglow yellow dashed road, paired with Poppins and Work Sans — a brand that reads as confident and easy to scan at a glance while driving.

Design

04

We tested the MVP live, in the car, with eight students: most recalled the traffic-code trivia correctly afterwards and didn't find the audio distracting while driving.

Usertesting

05

Mounted on the dashboard and tried out on a real route, RoadMate went from Figma prototype to something you could actually drive with.

End product

End product

“A few seconds of audio, and the rules you forgot come right back to you.”
