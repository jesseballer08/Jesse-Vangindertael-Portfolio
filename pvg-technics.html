PVG Technics

Web design · Webflow · Family business

My father runs PVG Technics, a family business near Leuven specialising in sanitary work, climate installations and bathroom renovations. I designed and built his website in Webflow — from the concept and content down to the responsive build — giving the business a clear, professional home to show off finished bathrooms and bring in new clients.

Process

01

A trustworthy, no-nonsense first impression: the headline states what PVG Technics does and for how long, backed immediately by a photo of their own work.

Concept

02

I grouped the services into three expandable sections — Klimaat & Energie, Sanitair & Loodgieterij, Badkamerrenovaties — so visitors can scan quickly or dig into what they need.

Content

03

A minimal layout in white, grey and the brand’s blue lets the renovation photography carry the page instead of competing with it.

Design

04

Built and tested in Webflow across breakpoints, since most visitors find a local plumber through their phone.

Responsive build

05

The site is live today and used by PVG Technics to show their work and bring in new client requests.

End product

End product

“A website that lets the bathrooms speak for themselves.”
