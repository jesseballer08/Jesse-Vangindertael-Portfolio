Schaduwen

VR installation · Immersive Experience Lab 3 · with Arthur De Klerck

Starting from Delphine Somers’ painting Schaduwen, Arthur De Klerck and I built a virtual story around it in Unity for Meta VR, woven through a set of tarot cards. In the experience, built for Museum M in Leuven, the visitor — Sophie — works through self-reflection as the scenario moves from self-doubt after a separation toward letting go and a new direction.

Process

01

Shadows as a metaphor for a mysterious, emotional message about self-reflection and personal growth — built as an interactive VR experience rather than a passive film.

Concept

02

I designed every tarot card myself. Drawing a card in VR reveals its own explanation, guiding the visitor through what it means for her story.

Interaction

03

Built in Unity and experienced on a Meta Quest VR headset, with the environment and characters modelled in Blender and Meshy.

Technology

04

Set in Museum M, Leopold Vanderkelenstraat 28, 3000 Leuven — where modern architecture meets a historic facade, just like the contrast between light and shadow in the installation.

Location

05

The project is meant to give an interactive way of looking at a painting — in this case, tarot cards paired with the painting Schaduwen.

End product
